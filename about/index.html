I am a research associate at UCLouvain, where I also teach planetary geophysics as a guest lecturer, and a guest researcher at the Royal Observatory of Belgium. I study the dynamics of rotating fluids: the liquid core of the Earth and how it couples to the planet's rotation and magnetic field, the subsurface oceans of icy moons, and the fluid layers of other planets and stars.

Much of this work relies on numerical models. With colleagues, I develop Kore, a spectral code for rotating fluids and magnetohydrodynamics.

I obtained my PhD in cosmology and general relativity at the University of Namur in 2015, before turning to the interiors of planets.

I also enjoy explaining science to a broad audience, on my blog and elsewhere. In 2024, the Royal Academy of Belgium awarded me the Mailly Prize for the popularisation of astronomy.
